Our Story

A small pot with a big purpose.

EcoPot began with a simple question: why do healthy plants start life in plastic that lasts for centuries? We are building a better alternative. Our pots nurture your plants and then return safely to the earth.

How It Works

From seed to soil in four simple steps

Fill

Add potting soil to your EcoPot, leaving a little space at the top.

Sow

Plant your seed or seedling and water it gently.

Grow

Keep it in good light and water regularly as your plant thrives.

Transplant

Place the whole pot into the garden or a bigger container. It biodegrades naturally.
